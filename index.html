01 / Welcome

Welcome to Honkapirtti – A home in the peace of the countryside.

Honkapirtti is much more to us than just a place to live. It is a home surrounded by a yard and garden that we have lovingly built over the years — a place where nature, country life and an unhurried pace are part of everyday life.

Read more

It all started with a summer cottage. Honkapirtti was originally our family’s holiday retreat, where our children could grow up surrounded by nature. As the children grew older, we decided to make a bigger change and move here permanently. Since the original cottage was a small sauna cabin, we built a new log house in the yard. Honkapirtti has been our permanent home since 2018.

Because we enjoyed living here so much, the idea was born to open this beloved place to others as well. In 2020, the outbuilding in our yard was given a new life as guest accommodation.

Honkapirtti is an open grounds and garden area where we warmly welcome guests who enjoy nature, gardening, peaceful countryside and beautiful surroundings. We want to share with you the atmosphere that makes this place home for us.

We have gradually developed the yard and garden over the years. Here, you can wander around, sit down for a moment in the garden, take in the tranquility of nature, or simply be and breathe. In the summer, the original pond on the grounds invites you for a refreshing swim, and the surrounding forests offer great opportunities for peaceful walks.

At the edge of the forest, we also have our own lean-to shelter, where you can take an excursion to cook sausages over the open fire. The lean-to is a wonderful spot to pause in the middle of nature, light a campfire and enjoy a stress-free moment.

In 2024, we added a small garden sauna to the yard, and both we and our guests get to enjoy its warm steam and calm atmosphere.

You are welcome to come to Honkapirtti just as you are. There is no need to perform or accomplish anything here. You can stay overnight in the cabin, explore the grounds and garden, head out onto the forest trails, visit the lean-to shelter, grill sausages, take a dip in the pond, warm up in the sauna, or simply sit in total peace and take in the surrounding countryside.

If you wish, you can also order breakfast, a light evening meal or soup from us. Together, we can tailor your stay to be just the way you want it.

Honkapirtti is a small slice of countryside peace — an open yard and garden where you can pause for a while. We welcome you to join us and enjoy nature, the garden, the rural scenery, and the authentic atmosphere of the place.

02 / The host

I’m Tiinu, your hostess at Honkapirtin Piha.

I’m originally from Karelia, but I’ve made Southwest Finland my home. I’m someone who genuinely loves nature, meeting people, and trying out new things.

By nature, I’m an enthusiast and an explorer. Staying active, wandering in the forest, picking mushrooms, and riding my bike have always been part of who I am. At Honkapirtin Piha I've also come to love gardening.

My wish is for everyone who steps into our yard to feel warmly welcome and completely free to be themselves. Whether you want to chat, share a moment with others, or just enjoy some quiet time on your own—you're free to do things your own way.

You can simply pause, enjoy the surrounding nature, sit back with a quiet cup of coffee, fry up a batch of pancakes, or just be. For me, that’s what creates the true magic of Honkapirtti.
